Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about India's top tour management app for travel agencies & Umrah tour operators

Which is the best tour management app in India for Umrah and Hajj tour operators?

Sadiq E Safar by Jilani Tech is India's leading tour management app designed for Hajj, Umrah, and travel agency operators. It provides automated passport OCR scanning, Makkah & Madinah hotel room allocation, PAX pilgrim lists, invoices, receipts, and payment tracking in one unified cloud system starting at ₹499/month.

Can I manage Makkah and Madinah hotel room allocation in Sadiq E Safar?

Yes! Sadiq E Safar includes an intelligent Hotel & Room Allotment engine. You can allocate pilgrims across Quad, Triple, Double, and Sharing rooms in Makkah & Madinah hotels, generate rooming lists for hotel managers, and track check-in and check-out dates without confusion.

How does the Passport Scanner benefit Umrah & Hajj travel agencies?

Instead of manually typing passport numbers for hundreds of pilgrims, Sadiq E Safar's built-in Passport OCR Scanner extracts full name, passport number, birth date, expiry, and nationality in seconds. It eliminates clerical errors and saves hours of operational work.

Does Sadiq E Safar work on mobile (Android & iOS) as well as desktop?

Yes, Sadiq E Safar is a cross-platform cloud application accessible via web browser on desktop and mobile, as well as dedicated apps for Android and iOS devices, allowing tour leaders to manage tours on the go in Saudi Arabia.
